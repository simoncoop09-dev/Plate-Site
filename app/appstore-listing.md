**Description:**
Plate is the first meal planner built around the reality of GLP-1 medications.

Your week on a GLP-1 has a rhythm — rough right after your shot, hungrier by
the weekend. Plate plans around it.

• 7-DAY MEAL PLANS THAT FOLLOW YOUR CYCLE
Tell Plate your injection day once. Gentle, cold, protein-first meals for the
rough days. Fuller meals when your appetite returns.

• LIBBY, YOUR AI NUTRITION COMPANION
Ask anything — what to eat on a nausea day, how to hit protein with a tiny
appetite, what to order at a restaurant. Warm, specific, judgment-free.

• AUTOMATIC GROCERY LISTS
Your whole week's ingredients, deduplicated and sorted by aisle, with quick
links to shop online.

• A 10-SECOND MORNING RITUAL
One tap — "How's your stomach today?" — and your day's meals adjust.

• SHOT-DAY REMINDERS
Plate quietly preps you the evening before your shot day and checks in the
morning of.

Plate provides general nutrition information only — not medical advice.
Always consult your doctor about your medication and diet. Intended for
adults 18+.
