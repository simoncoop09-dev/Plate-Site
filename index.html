Why we built it

The problems no one prepares you for

Every GLP-1 forum has the same three posts on repeat. Plate is built around them.

01

"Nothing sounds edible"

Food aversion and nausea make normal meals impossible, so people end up living on crackers. Plate leans into cold, bland, protein-dense options on the days you need them — and saves the real food for the days you can enjoy it.

02

"I'm losing muscle, not just fat"

A tiny appetite makes protein targets genuinely hard to hit. Plate sets a goal based on your body, shows it as one simple ring, and helps you front-load protein on the days your appetite comes back.

03

"I can't think about food anymore"

Decision fatigue is real when eating stops being fun. Plate decides for you — a plan that already accounts for your medication, your shot day, and how you actually feel this morning.
