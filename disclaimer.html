Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Plate is not a medical service. Nothing on this site or in the Plate app — including anything said by Libby, our AI nutrition assistant — is medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always talk to your doctor, prescriber, or a registered dietitian before changing your diet, medication, or exercise.

What Plate is

Plate is a general nutrition and meal-planning tool designed for people who are taking GLP-1 medications such as Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, and Zepbound. We provide general food information, meal ideas, and protein-tracking tools.

What Plate is not

  • Plate does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or condition.
  • Plate does not provide dosing guidance. We will never tell you to start, stop, change, skip, or adjust a medication.
  • Plate does not replace your doctor, prescriber, pharmacist, or dietitian.
  • Plate is not a substitute for emergency care.

About Libby, our AI assistant

Libby is an artificial intelligence tool. Like all AI systems, Libby can be wrong, incomplete, or out of date, and may produce information that sounds confident but is inaccurate. Do not rely on Libby for any decision affecting your health. Verify anything important with a qualified professional.

Individual results vary

Nutrition needs differ from person to person based on medical history, medication, dose, allergies, and many other factors. General information on this site may not be appropriate for you. We make no promise of any particular outcome, including weight loss, muscle retention, or symptom relief.

When to seek medical care

Contact your healthcare provider promptly if you experience persistent vomiting, severe abdominal pain, signs of dehydration, or any symptom that worries you. If you believe you are having a medical emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the United States) immediately. Do not wait, and do not use Plate or Libby in place of emergency care.
